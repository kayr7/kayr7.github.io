// BRIEFING

The world is warming. You govern one region of Earth until 2100. Each turn is 5 years. You have two jobs, and they pull at each other:

MITIGATE — cut emissions, build clean energy, capture carbon. But know the trap: your region is a slice of world emissions, and going clean alone barely moves the thermometer while costing you dearly. The way out is that resolve is contagious — WORLD RESOLVE rises when regions visibly cut and collapses when they free-ride, and your CREDIBILITY (clean at home) decides whether your summits move anyone. Lead and the world follows; defect and it defects with you. A prisoner's dilemma — except everyone shares one cell.

ADAPT — sea walls, heat shelters, food & water security, migration readiness. Warming already under way will hit; preparation decides whether people live.

ECONOMY — growth funds everything but can raise emissions. Green research makes clean energy cheaper for the whole planet.

The DEGCON level tracks global warming: DEGCON 5 is caution, DEGCON 1 is catastrophe. Every round hits harder than the last, and past certain degrees the Earth crosses tipping points that cannot be undone. Beyond +4.0 °C civilization does not make it. Then everybody dies — and nobody wins.

Watch the top of the screen: the Gt/yr → +°C 2100 projection shows where the world is headed right now — buy clean energy and watch it fall. The red death counter shows what hesitation costs. Cities can be lost — to the sea, to the heat, to war — permanently.

When regions fail, their people move. Set your border stance: open doors need capacity, closed doors kill at the fence and breed tension. Past the breaking point, tension becomes war — cities burn, refugees multiply, emissions climb. Armed forces deter, peace talks defuse; choose.

You can't afford everything — a strategy is what you choose not to build. Score = people kept alive & thriving + a world that is still standing. You can win the century and still lose the planet. Don't.
